Version 8.2 Prototype

Random three-letter, four-letter, and five-letter words. Ninety seconds.

How to Play

  1. Each round has one three-letter word, one four-letter word, and one five-letter word.
  2. Drag the active letter into any word space.
  3. A green letter is in the correct position. A red letter belongs in a different position.
  4. Drag a board letter into an empty space to move it.
  5. Drag a board letter onto another letter to swap them.
  6. Drop the active tray letter onto an occupied space to replace it. The displaced letter returns to the tray.
  7. Complete as many rounds as possible in ninety seconds.
  8. Three-letter words score 100 points, four-letter words score 250 points, and five-letter words score 500 points.
