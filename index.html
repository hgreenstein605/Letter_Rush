How to play

  1. Solve a 3-letter, 4-letter, and 5-letter word at the same time.
  2. Only one letter appears at a time. Drag it into the exact space where it belongs.
  3. The tray uses only letters from the active words. Dropping the tray letter onto an occupied space returns the replaced letter to the tray. Dragging one board letter onto another still swaps them.
Complete as many rounds as possible in 90 seconds. A mistake-free round earns 300 bonus points.
